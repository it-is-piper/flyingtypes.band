## bio

Jeff, Nick, and Piper met playing in an afro-cuban jazz band and free jazz combo in 2008.

They have stayed connected as each has taken their own musical journey.

Their music combines influences from jazz, bluegrass, folk, country, and gospel into a fresh mosaic.

## the band

**Piper Maxine Baker** is a keyboardist based in Somerville, MA.

**Nick Sokol** is a tenor saxophonist and mandolinist living in Bellingham, WA.

**Jeff Chang** is an alto saxophonist and band director out of Federal Way, WA.
